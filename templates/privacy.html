Privacy Policy

How Spendly handles your information.

1. Data We Collect

We collect the information you give us directly: your name, email address and password when you register, and the expense details you enter, such as amount, category, date and description.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used only to run Spendly for you: to sign you in, store your expenses, and show you summaries and spending patterns. We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in a database on the server that runs Spendly. Passwords are stored in hashed form, not as plain text. You can edit or delete your expenses at any time. No storage system is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser may contact Google's servers when pages load. We do not share your expense data with any other third-party service.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want your data removed, please contact us at support@spendly.example.
